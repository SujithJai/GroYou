# GroYou Content Editing Guide

Edit most website text, images, links, founder details, social links, and pricing from:

`src/data/siteContent.ts`

Common edits:

- Change logo path: update `assets.logo`
- Change founder images: update `assets.founder1`, `assets.founder2`, `assets.founder3`
- Change phone, email, form, domain: update `brand`
- Change floating social popup links: update `socialLinks`
- Change Founder Story milestones: update `founderStory.milestones`
- Change founder cards: update `founders`
- Change pricing cards: update `pricingPackages`

Put new image files in `public/` and reference them like `/your-file.png`.
